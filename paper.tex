\begin{abstract}
We introduce oblique hyperplane projection deletion, an exact rank-preserving dimension-reduction operator on bilinear matrix multiplication algorithms. Combined with a pivot-exchange reduction step on linear dependencies among rank-$1$ terms, this method improves known characteristic-$0$ bilinear rank upper bounds for 58 matrix multiplication formats relative to the monotone-closed state of the art as of November 24, 2026 (\texttt{fmm\_digest} commit \texttt{2daba01}), including 10 formats with $\max(m,n,p)\le 16$ and 14 schemes with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}[1/2]$. Every scheme is independently verified over exact $\mathbb{Q}$ by checking all Brent equations via the Chinese Remainder Theorem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A bilinear algorithm of rank $R$ for multiplying an $m\times n$ matrix $A$ by an $n\times p$ matrix $B$ over a commutative ring $K$ is specified by matrices $U \in K^{R\times mn}$, $V \in K^{R\times np}$, and $W \in K^{R\times mp}$ satisfying the Brent identity
\begin{equation}
\sum_{r=1}^R \langle U_r, A\rangle \langle V_r, B\rangle W_r = AB
\qquad \forall A \in K^{m\times n},\; B \in K^{n\times p},
\end{equation}
where $\langle X, Y\rangle = \sum_{i,j} X_{ij} Y_{ij}$ denotes the entrywise Frobenius inner product and $U_r, V_r, W_r$ are reshaped to $m\times n$, $n\times p$, and $m\times p$ respectively. By cyclic invariance and transposition of the trilinear trace form $\operatorname{Tr}(ABC^T)$, any scheme for $\langle m\times n\times p : R\rangle$ induces an exact scheme of the same rank for every permutation of $(m,n,p)$; we therefore index formats by their canonical sorted triple $m \le n \le p$.

Recent exact matrix multiplication constructions---including LITA's rational square schemes for $13^3$ through $32^3$~\cite{lita2026}, flip-graph catalogs by Perminov~\cite{perminov2026} and Canli~\cite{quantavane2026}, and systematic coordinate-deletion sweeps by Sidebottom~\cite{sidebottom2026}---have lowered known ranks across hundreds of formats tracked in Sedoglavic's Lille Catalogue~\cite{sedoglavic2026}. However, prior deletion pipelines exclusively apply coordinate deletions (zeroing a row or column $e_k$ of $A$ or $B$), which do not align with non-axis-aligned linear subspaces present in structured schemes.

In this note we generalize coordinate deletion to oblique hyperplane projections parameterized by $(\alpha, \beta)$ with $\alpha^T \beta \neq 0$, and pair it with a pivot-exchange search for rank reduction, yielding 58 strictly improved characteristic-$0$ algorithms.

\section{Oblique Hyperplane Projection Deletion}
\begin{theorem}[Oblique Hyperplane Projection Deletion]
Let $(U, V, W)$ be an exact bilinear scheme of rank $R$ for $\langle m\times n\times p\rangle$ over a field $K$. Let $d \in \{m,n,p\}$ be the dimension of a chosen axis, and let $\alpha, \beta \in K^d$ be any vectors such that $\alpha^T \beta \neq 0$ (specifying the hyperplane $\ker(\alpha^T)$ and the complementary projection direction $\operatorname{span}(\beta)$). Choose any index $k \in \{0,\dots,d-1\}$ with $\alpha_k \neq 0$, let $J = \{0,\dots,d-1\}\setminus\{k\}$, and define the embedding $E \in K^{d\times (d-1)}$ and oblique projection $\Pi \in K^{(d-1)\times d}$ by
\begin{equation}
E_J = I_{d-1}, \quad E_{k,j} = -\frac{\alpha_j}{\alpha_k}\;\; (j \in J),
\qquad
\Pi = R_J\!\left(I_d - \frac{\beta \alpha^T}{\alpha^T \beta}\right),
\end{equation}
where $R_J \in K^{(d-1)\times d}$ selects the rows indexed by $J$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\alpha^T E = 0$, $\Pi \beta = 0$, and $\Pi E = I_{d-1}$.
\item The transformed triplets below are exact bilinear schemes of rank at most $R$:
\begin{align*}
\text{Axis } 0\;(m\to m-1): &\quad (E^T U_r,\; V_r,\; \Pi W_r)_{r=1}^R \quad \text{for } \langle (m-1)\times n\times p\rangle, \\
\text{Axis } 1\;(n\to n-1): &\quad (U_r \Pi^T,\; E^T V_r,\; W_r)_{r=1}^R \quad \text{for } \langle m\times (n-1)\times p\rangle, \\
\text{Axis } 2\;(p\to p-1): &\quad (U_r,\; V_r E,\; W_r \Pi^T)_{r=1}^R \quad \text{for } \langle m\times n\times (p-1)\rangle.
\end{align*}
\item Every rank-$1$ term whose corresponding slice matrix has row space (or column space) contained in $\operatorname{span}(\alpha)$ or $\operatorname{span}(\beta)$ vanishes identically and is eliminated.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For property~(1), $(\alpha^T E)_j = \alpha_j + \alpha_k(-\alpha_j/\alpha_k) = 0$ for all $j\in J$; hence $\Pi E = R_J(E - \frac{\beta(\alpha^T E)}{\alpha^T\beta}) = R_J E = E_J = I_{d-1}$. Moreover, $\Pi \beta = R_J(\beta - \frac{\beta(\alpha^T\beta)}{\alpha^T\beta}) = 0$.

For property~(2), consider Axis~2 ($p\to p-1$). For arbitrary $A \in K^{m\times n}$ and $B' \in K^{n\times(p-1)}$, apply the original scheme to $A$ and $B = B' E^T \in K^{n\times p}$, and multiply the output on the right by $\Pi^T$. Using $\langle V_r, B' E^T\rangle = \operatorname{Tr}(V_r^T B' E^T) = \operatorname{Tr}((V_r E)^T B') = \langle V_r E, B'\rangle$ together with $(\Pi E)^T = I_{p-1}$, we obtain
\[
\sum_{r=1}^R \langle U_r, A\rangle \langle V_r E, B'\rangle (W_r \Pi^T)
= \left(\sum_{r=1}^R \langle U_r, A\rangle \langle V_r, B' E^T\rangle W_r\right)\Pi^T
= (A B' E^T)\Pi^T = A B' (\Pi E)^T = A B'.
\]
Axes~0 and~1 follow identically by setting $A = E A'$ (and multiplying the output on the left by $\Pi$, using $\Pi E = I_{m-1}$) and by setting $A = A'\Pi$, $B = E B'$ (using $\Pi E = I_{n-1}$). Property~(3) follows directly from $\alpha^T E = 0$ and $\Pi\beta = 0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Structural Alignment with LITA Squares]
Standard coordinate deletion is the special case $\alpha = \beta = e_k$. On odd LITA squares $q = 2s+1$ ($13^3, 15^3, 17^3, 19^3, 21^3, 23^3$), taking $\alpha = \beta = (1,\dots,1,-(s-2),1,\dots,1)$ directly annihilates $4(s-1)s$ rank-$1$ products (e.g., $84, 112, 144, 180, 220, 264$ terms), outperforming coordinate deletion by $s-1$ on the first step alone. On even LITA squares $q = 2s$ ($14^3, 16^3, 18^3, 20^3, 22^3$), coordinate deletion directly annihilates $0$ rank-$1$ products, whereas symmetric oblique projection along half-period vectors $e_i \pm e_{i+s}$ directly eliminates between $23$ and $35$ terms and induces further rank reductions in the subsequent step.
\end{remark}

\section{Pivot-Exchange Reduction and Exact CRT Verification}
After an oblique hyperplane projection deletion, linear dependencies among the rank-$1$ factor products allow further exact rank reductions. Consider the $R \times (mn\cdot np)$ matrix $M_{UV}$ whose $r$-th row is the Kronecker product $\operatorname{vec}(U_r) \otimes \operatorname{vec}(V_r)$ (and analogously $M_{UW}$ and $M_{VW}$). Whenever $\sum_{r=1}^R c_r (U_r \otimes V_r) = 0$ with $c_f = 1$, product $f$ can be removed while updating $W_r \leftarrow W_r - c_r W_f$ for all $r \neq f$, leaving the bilinear map unchanged.

More generally, if the left nullspace of $M_{UV}$ has dimension $d$, any choice of $d$ independent pivot indices $F \subset \{1,\dots,R\}$ eliminates those $d$ products and replaces each remaining output matrix $W_r$ ($r \notin F$) by a linear combination of $W_r$ and $\{W_f : f \in F\}$. While every valid pivot set $F$ removes $d$ terms directly, different choices of $F$ can cause additional updated matrices $W'_r$ to vanish identically. We compute the left nullspace modulo a prime $P = 2^{20}-3$ and perform single-element basis exchanges $F \mapsto (F\setminus\{p\})\cup\{k\}$ to maximize the number of additional vanishing rows $W'_r = 0$, often removing $3d$--$7d$ terms in total.

To guarantee exact characteristic-$0$ validity without floating-point approximation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every selected deletion sequence and pivot set $F$ is replayed in exact rational arithmetic (\texttt{fractions.Fraction}). Nullspace bases are lifted to $\mathbb{Q}$ from three primes ($P_1 = 2^{20}-3$, $P_2 = 2^{20}-5$, $P_3 = 2^{20}-17$, product $>1.15\times 10^{18}$) via the Chinese Remainder Theorem and Wang's rational reconstruction, and the linear dependency $\sum_r c_r (U_r \otimes V_r) = 0$ (or its $UW$/$VW$ analogue) is checked identically over $\mathbb{Q}$ prior to every elimination.
\item Each resulting scheme is oriented to canonical format $m\le n\le p$ and cleared of denominators by positive integers $(d_U, d_V, d_W)$ so that $\tilde{U} = d_U U$, $\tilde{V} = d_V V$, and $\tilde{W} = d_W W$ are integer matrices (with $d_U = d_V = 1$). Setting $D = d_U d_V d_W$, a standalone verifier selects distinct 30-bit primes ($999{,}999{,}937, 999{,}999{,}929, \dots$) whose product strictly exceeds $2\bigl(D + \sum_{r=1}^R \|\tilde{U}_r\|_\infty \|\tilde{V}_r\|_\infty \|\tilde{W}_r\|_\infty\bigr)$, verifies all $(mn)(np)(mp)$ Brent equations modulo every prime, and runs exact rational matrix-multiplication checks together with a single-entry perturbation test.
\end{enumerate}
