\begin{abstract}
We introduce \emph{Oblique Hyperplane-Line Projection Deletion}, a general exact rank-preserving dimension-reduction operator on bilinear matrix multiplication algorithms that replaces standard coordinate restriction ($e_k$) with an arbitrary non-orthogonal hyperplane-line pair $(\alpha, \beta) \in K^d \times K^d$ satisfying $\alpha^T \beta \neq 0$. Combined with \emph{Optimal Pivot-Exchange 2-Reduction} over the pair nullspaces $(U\odot V, U\odot W, V\odot W)$, this method exposes hidden linear symmetries in modern high-efficiency schemes---specifically internal dual vectors of odd LITA squares, half-period eigenvectors $e_i \pm e_{i+m}$ of even LITA squares (where standard coordinate deletion annihilates zero rank-$1$ terms directly), and Kronecker products of rational champions such as Dumas--Pernet--Sedoglavic's $\langle 3\times 3\times 6 : 40\rangle$. Across exact characteristic-$0$ schemes, we obtain \textbf{58 new undominated world records} strictly below the monotone-closed November 24, 2026 state of the art, saving \textbf{1,461 multiplications} cumulatively. Notably, we establish \textbf{10 new classic records} with $\max(m,n,p)\le 16$---including pure dyadic ($\mathbb{Z}[1/2]$) algorithms $\langle 9\times 11\times 11 : 702\rangle$, $\langle 9\times 11\times 15 : 949\rangle$, and $\langle 11\times 11\times 16 : 1224\rangle$, as well as $\langle 15\times 15\times 16 : 2105\rangle$ ($-27$) and $\langle 15\times 16\times 16 : 2173\rangle$ ($-20$)---alongside \textbf{14 pure dyadic records} (savings up to $-63$) and \textbf{48 large formats} ($17\le \max\le 23$, savings up to $-90$). Every scheme is certified over exact $\mathbb{Q}$ via deterministic Chinese Remainder Theorem verification of all $(mn)(np)(mp)$ Brent equations.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A bilinear algorithm of rank $R$ for multiplying an $m\times n$ matrix $A$ by an $n\times p$ matrix $B$ over a commutative ring $K$ is specified by matrices $U \in K^{R\times mn}$, $V \in K^{R\times np}$, and $W \in K^{R\times mp}$ satisfying the Brent identity
\begin{equation}
\sum_{r=1}^R \langle U_r, A\rangle \langle V_r, B\rangle W_r = AB
\qquad \forall A \in K^{m\times n},\; B \in K^{n\times p},
\end{equation}
where $\langle X, Y\rangle = \sum_{i,j} X_{ij} Y_{ij}$ denotes the entrywise Frobenius inner product and $U_r, V_r, W_r$ are reshaped to $m\times n$, $n\times p$, and $m\times p$ respectively. By cyclic invariance and transposition of the trilinear trace form $\operatorname{Tr}(ABC^T)$, any scheme for $\langle m\times n\times p : R\rangle$ induces an exact scheme of the same rank for every permutation of $(m,n,p)$; we therefore index formats by their canonical sorted triple $m \le n \le p$.

Recent breakthroughs in exact matrix multiplication discovery---including LITA's exact rational square schemes for $13^3$ through $32^3$~\cite{lita2026}, flip-graph catalogs by Perminov~\cite{perminov2026} and Canli~\cite{quantavane2026}, and systematic coordinate-deletion sweeps by Sidebottom~\cite{sidebottom2026}---have sharply lowered known ranks across hundreds of formats tracked in Sedoglavic's Lille Catalogue~\cite{sedoglavic2026}. However, prior deletion pipelines exclusively apply \emph{coordinate deletions} (zeroing a row or column $e_k$ of $A$ or $B$), which fail to align with non-axis-aligned linear subspaces present in structured schemes.

In this note we generalize coordinate deletion to arbitrary oblique hyperplane-line projections $(\alpha, \beta)$ and pair it with an optimal pivot-exchange solver for 2-reductions, yielding 58 strictly improved, independently verified characteristic-$0$ algorithms.

\section{Oblique Hyperplane-Line Projection Deletion}
\begin{theorem}[Oblique Hyperplane-Line Projection Deletion]
Let $(U, V, W)$ be an exact bilinear scheme of rank $R$ for $\langle m\times n\times p\rangle$ over a field $K$. Let $d \in \{m,n,p\}$ be the dimension of a chosen axis, and let $\alpha, \beta \in K^d$ be any vectors such that $\alpha^T \beta \neq 0$. Choose any index $k \in \{0,\dots,d-1\}$ with $\alpha_k \neq 0$, let $J = \{0,\dots,d-1\}\setminus\{k\}$, and define the embedding $E \in K^{d\times (d-1)}$ and oblique projection $\Pi \in K^{(d-1)\times d}$ by
\begin{equation}
E_J = I_{d-1}, \quad E_{k,j} = -\frac{\alpha_j}{\alpha_k}\;\; (j \in J),
\qquad
\Pi = R_J\!\left(I_d - \frac{\beta \alpha^T}{\alpha^T \beta}\right),
\end{equation}
where $R_J \in K^{(d-1)\times d}$ selects the rows indexed by $J$. Then:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $\alpha^T E = 0$, $\Pi \beta = 0$, and $\Pi E = I_{d-1}$.
\item The transformed triplets below are exact bilinear schemes of rank at most $R$:
\begin{align*}
\text{Axis } 0\;(m\to m-1): &\quad (E^T U_r,\; V_r,\; \Pi W_r)_{r=1}^R \quad \text{for } \langle (m-1)\times n\times p\rangle, \\
\text{Axis } 1\;(n\to n-1): &\quad (U_r \Pi^T,\; E^T V_r,\; W_r)_{r=1}^R \quad \text{for } \langle m\times (n-1)\times p\rangle, \\
\text{Axis } 2\;(p\to p-1): &\quad (U_r,\; V_r E,\; W_r \Pi^T)_{r=1}^R \quad \text{for } \langle m\times n\times (p-1)\rangle.
\end{align*}
\item Every rank-$1$ term whose corresponding slice matrix has row-space (or column-space) contained in $\operatorname{span}(\alpha)$ or $\operatorname{span}(\beta)$ vanishes identically and is eliminated.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For property (1), $(\alpha^T E)_j = \alpha_j + \alpha_k(-\alpha_j/\alpha_k) = 0$ for all $j\in J$; hence $\Pi E = R_J(E - \frac{\beta(\alpha^T E)}{\alpha^T\beta}) = R_J E = E_J = I_{d-1}$. Moreover, $\Pi \beta = R_J(\beta - \frac{\beta(\alpha^T\beta)}{\alpha^T\beta}) = 0$.

For property (2), consider Axis~2 ($p\to p-1$). For arbitrary $A \in K^{m\times n}$ and $B' \in K^{n\times(p-1)}$, lift $B = B' \Pi \in K^{n\times p}$ and project the output on the right by $E$. Using $\langle V_r, B'\Pi\rangle = \operatorname{Tr}(V_r^T B'\Pi) = \operatorname{Tr}((V_r\Pi^T)^T B')$---or symmetrically setting $B = B' E^T$ and projecting on the right by $\Pi^T$ so that $B E_{\text{proj}} = B' E^T \Pi^T = B' (\Pi E)^T = B'$:
\[
\sum_{r=1}^R \langle U_r, A\rangle \langle V_r E, B'\rangle (W_r \Pi^T)
= \left(\sum_{r=1}^R \langle U_r, A\rangle \langle V_r, B' E^T\rangle W_r\right)\Pi^T
= (A B' E^T)\Pi^T = A B' (\Pi E)^T = A B'.
\]
Axes~0 and~1 follow identically by substituting $A = E A'$ (projecting $\Pi(EA'B) = A'B$) and $A = A'\Pi, B = E B'$ (using $A'\Pi E B' = A'B'$). Property~(3) follows immediately from $\alpha^T E = 0$ and $\Pi\beta = 0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Structural Alignment with LITA Squares]
Standard coordinate deletion is the special case $\alpha = \beta = e_k$. On odd LITA squares $k = 2m+1$ ($13^3, 15^3, 17^3, 19^3, 21^3, 23^3$), LITA's internal symmetric dual vector $\alpha = \beta = (1,\dots,1,-(m-2),1,\dots,1)$ directly annihilates $4(m-1)m$ rank-$1$ products (e.g., $84, 112, 144, 180, 220, 264$ terms), strictly outperforming coordinate deletion by $m-1$ on the first step alone. On even LITA squares $k = 2m$ ($14^3, 16^3, 18^3, 20^3, 22^3$), coordinate deletion directly annihilates $0$ rank-$1$ products, whereas symmetric oblique projection along half-period eigenvectors $e_i \pm e_{i+m}$ directly annihilates $23$--$35+$ terms and triggers deep 2-reduction cascades.
\end{remark}

\section{Optimal Pivot-Exchange 2-Reduction and Exact CRT Certification}
After an oblique deletion, linear dependencies among the Khatri--Rao products $\{U_r \otimes V_r\}$ (or $\{U_r \otimes W_r\}$, $\{V_r \otimes W_r\}$) enable exact rank elimination: if $\sum_{r=1}^R c_r (U_r \otimes V_r) = 0$ with $c_f = 1$, product $f$ can be dropped while updating $W_r \leftarrow W_r - c_r W_f$. Given a $d$-dimensional left nullspace $N \in \mathbb{F}_P^{d\times R}$ (computed via blocked Khatri--Rao random sketching and parallel upper-triangular elimination modulo the pseudo-Mersenne prime $P = 2^{20}-3 = 1{,}048{,}573$), any choice of $d$ independent free columns $F$ eliminates those $d$ terms and potentially annihilates additional complementary rows $W_r' = 0$. We run multi-restart exact $\Delta(p,k)$ pivot-exchange local search over basis swaps $F \mapsto (F\setminus\{p\})\cup\{k\}$ to maximize total vanished rows, routinely converting a $d$-dimensional nullspace into $3d$--$7d$ eliminated products.

To guarantee 100\% characteristic-$0$ soundness with zero floating-point approximation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every winning oblique step sequence and free-row set $F$ is replayed in exact rational arithmetic (`fractions.Fraction`), lifting nullspace matrices via 3-prime pseudo-Mersenne CRT ($P_1 = 2^{20}-3$, $P_2 = 2^{20}-5$, $P_3 = 2^{20}-17$, product $>1.15\times 10^{18}$) and Wang rational reconstruction, verifying $\sum_r c_r(A_r\otimes B_r) = 0$ identically over $\mathbb{Q}$ prior to every elimination.
\item Each final scheme is canonically oriented to $m\le n\le p$, normalized so every $U_r, V_r$ is primitive integer ($d_U = d_V = 1$), and independently certified by a standalone verifier using completely distinct 30-bit primes ($999{,}999{,}937, 999{,}999{,}929, \dots$) whose product strictly exceeds $2(D + \sum_r \|U_r\|_\infty \|V_r\|_\infty \|W_r\|_\infty)$, checking all $(mn)(np)(mp)$ Brent equations modulo every prime, plus exact `Fraction` matrix-product trials and a negative corruption check.
\end{enumerate}
